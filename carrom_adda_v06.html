Rules
  • Pocket one of your pieces and you shoot again.
  • Pocket the red queen, then cover her with one of your own pieces on a later shot (both on the same shot doesn’t count), or she goes back to the centre.
  • If your shot leaves the queen or an opponent’s piece touching or behind their baseline, it goes back to the centre, stacked on whatever is there.
  • A stack rides along on a very soft hit, but topples when hit harder.
  • Winner scores one point per opponent piece left on the board, plus 3 for a covered queen.
